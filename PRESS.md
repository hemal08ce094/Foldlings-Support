# Foldlings press kit

Foldlings is a small, calm game about pressing bits of nature together. Start with Water, Fire, Earth and Air; find 58 things in all, from Steam and Snow to a Snowman, a Butterfly and a Polar Bear. Every element is a hand-drawn creature that blinks and breathes.

- iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch (discoveries sync)
- Hints that start from what you're holding, and on-device Apple Intelligence clues
- Free. No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no tracking
