# Foldlings Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 28 September 2026_

Foldlings doesn't collect, store, or share any personal data.

- **No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.** The app has no third-party SDKs.
- **Your progress stays on your devices.** The elements you've found are saved on your iPhone or iPad, and on your Apple Watch if you use it. Phone and watch sync with each other directly through Apple's WatchConnectivity; nothing goes through a server.
- **Apple Intelligence runs on-device.** If you turn on nature clues, the text is generated by Apple's on-device model. Your game state never leaves your device.
- **Siri.** If you ask Siri about Foldlings, the app answers from the progress saved on your device. Apple's own Siri privacy terms apply to the request itself.
- **Motion.** The optional flip and shake gestures read the motion sensors while the game is on screen. Readings are used on the spot and never saved or sent.

Children can use Foldlings safely. It contains no in-app purchases, links out, or ways to contact other people.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
