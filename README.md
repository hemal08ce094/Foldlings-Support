# Foldlings — Support

Foldlings is a small game about pressing bits of nature together to see what they make. Water and Fire make Steam, Ice and Air make Snow, and it keeps going from there — 58 things to find in all.

## Help

**I'm stuck.** Tap "Stuck? Show a hint" under "What goes with…". The hint always starts from what you're holding. If that element has already made everything it can, the game tells you which one to pick up next. Sparkles on an element mean it makes something new with the one in your hand (you can switch sparkles off in Settings).

**How do I press two elements together?** Pick a second element, then drag it onto the one in your hand. You can also lay your phone face-down, or switch on "Shake to mix" in Settings.

**Does the Apple Watch app share my progress?** Yes. Discoveries sync both ways whenever the phone and watch can reach each other.

**Nature clues.** On devices with Apple Intelligence, hints can start as a short clue and each new discovery gets a one-line note. These are written on your device; nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in Settings.
